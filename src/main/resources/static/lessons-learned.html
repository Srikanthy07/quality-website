Lessons Learned

Continuous Process Improvement
Process Purpose

Capture, analyze, and apply lessons learned from projects to prevent problem recurrence and promote the adoption of best practices across the organization, helping teams continuously improve project execution and engineering quality.

Key Guidelines
  • Identify lessons learned at project milestones, major gateways, and project closure.
  • Perform structured root cause analysis to identify systemic improvements and best practices.
  • Classify lessons by process domain (e.g. System, Software, Project Management) and impact level.
  • Store captured knowledge in the centralized registry to ensure ease of retrieval.
  • Apply lessons learned to improve current and future process guidelines, checklist criteria, and training materials.
